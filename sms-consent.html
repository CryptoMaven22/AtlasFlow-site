SMS Consent & Messaging Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026 · AtlasFlow LLC · getatlasflow.io

AtlasFlow sends automated SMS messages on behalf of licensed real estate agents to their leads and past clients.

Agents are required by our Terms of Service to obtain your prior express written consent before entering your phone number into our platform.

What messages you may receive

  • Initial follow-up messages from the agent about a real estate inquiry
  • Follow-up messages if you have not yet responded
  • Transaction updates if you are in an active real estate transaction
  • Post-close check-in messages and market updates from your agent

Example message

Sample first message

Hi [Name], this is [Agent Name] from [Brokerage]. Thanks for your interest — I'd love to connect about your real estate needs. Is now a good time? Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one final confirmation and will not receive further messages. Other opt-out keywords: STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply. AtlasFlow does not charge recipients for messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. See our full Privacy Policy.
