AtlasFlow SMS PROGRAM SMS Consent & Messaging Policy Last updated: April 25, 2026 · Maven Group LLC · getatlasflow.io AtlasFlow sends automated SMS messages on behalf of licensed real estate agents to their leads and past clients. This page describes how our SMS program works, what recipients can expect, and how to opt out. Who Sends These Messages SMS messages sent through AtlasFlow are sent on behalf of the individual real estate agent whose name appears in the message. Maven Group LLC operates the technical infrastructure that delivers these messages via Twilio. What Messages You May Receive If your phone number was entered into AtlasFlow by a real estate agent, you may receive: • Initial follow-up messages from the agent about a real estate inquiry • Follow-up messages if you have not yet responded • Transaction updates and reminders if you are in an active real estate transaction • Post-close check-in messages and market updates from your agent Consent requirement AtlasFlow agents are required by our Terms of Service to obtain your prior express written consent before entering your phone number into our platform. If you believe you received a message without having consented, please contact us immediately at privacy@getatlasflow.io. Example Message SAMPLE FIRST MESSAGE FROM LAURA Hi [Name], this is [Agent Name] from [Brokerage]. Thanks for your interest — I'd love to connect about your real estate needs. Is now a good time? Reply STOP to unsubscribe. How to Opt Out You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one final confirmation message and will not receive further messages from that agent through AtlasFlow. Other opt-out keywords: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT To opt back in after opting out, reply START or YES. Help Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us directly at privacy@getatlasflow.io. Message Frequency and Costs Message frequency varies depending on your agent's settings and your level of engagement. Standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply. AtlasFlow does not charge recipients for messages. Supported Carriers AtlasFlow supports all major US carriers. Carrier support may vary for MVNO networks. Privacy Your phone number and message history are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
