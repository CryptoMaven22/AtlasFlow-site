Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026 · Maven Group LLC · getatlasflow.io

AtlasFlow does not sell, share, or expose your client data to third parties. Ever.

1. Who We Are

AtlasFlow is a product of Maven Group LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), a real estate automation platform accessible at getatlasflow.io. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Agent account information: name, email address, phone number, brokerage name, and billing information provided during signup.
  • Lead data: names and phone numbers of real estate leads entered by agents into the AtlasFlow platform on behalf of their clients.
  • SMS communication data: message content, delivery status, timestamps, and response data for SMS communications sent through our platform.
  • Usage data: how you interact with our platform, including log data and device information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Deliver the AtlasFlow service — including automated SMS follow-up, deal tracking, and referral nurturing
  • Send SMS messages on behalf of agents to their leads and clients
  • Notify agents of lead replies and platform activity
  • Process billing and manage your account
  • Improve and maintain the platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. SMS Messaging

AtlasFlow sends SMS messages on behalf of agents to real estate leads and past clients. By using our platform, agents represent and warrant that:

  • They have obtained prior express written consent from each lead or client before entering their phone number into AtlasFlow
  • All contacts have agreed to receive automated text messages related to real estate services
  • They will honor all opt-out requests promptly

Every SMS sequence includes opt-out instructions. Recipients may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from future messages. We honor all opt-out requests immediately and permanently.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on lead activity.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or your clients' data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted vendors who help us operate the platform (including Twilio for SMS delivery, Supabase for data storage, and Stripe for payments) who are contractually bound to protect your data
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with appropriate confidentiality protections

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your account credentials.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@getatlasflow.io. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request, except where retention is required by law.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getatlasflow.io.

9. Cookies

We use minimal, functional cookies to operate the platform. We do not use tracking cookies or sell data to advertising networks.

10. Children's Privacy

AtlasFlow is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active users of material changes by email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
