Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026 · AtlasFlow LLC · getatlasflow.io

AtlasFlow does not sell, share, or expose your client data to third parties. Ever.

1. Who We Are

AtlasFlow is a product of AtlasFlow LLC, a real estate automation platform accessible at getatlasflow.io.

2. Information We Collect

  • Agent account information: name, email, phone, brokerage, billing
  • Lead data: names and phone numbers entered by agents on behalf of their clients
  • SMS communication data: message content, delivery status, timestamps, response data
  • Usage data: how you interact with the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver the AtlasFlow service including automated SMS follow-up
  • Send SMS messages on behalf of agents to their leads and clients
  • Notify agents of lead replies and platform activity
  • Process billing and manage your account
  • Improve and maintain the platform

4. SMS Messaging

By using our platform, agents represent that they have obtained prior express written consent from each lead or client before entering their phone number. Every SMS sequence includes opt-out instructions. Recipients may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with trusted service providers (Twilio, Supabase, Stripe) who are contractually bound to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting privacy@getatlasflow.io. We will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
