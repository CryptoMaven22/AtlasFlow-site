Methodology

The Forge pathway

Eight connected stages move an opportunity from initial qualification to a defensible decision and delivery handoff.

Operating discipline

Design rules that protect trust

These rules keep every workflow understandable, traceable, and appropriately human-controlled.

01

Owner + source

Every input has an identifiable owner and source. Raw information remains distinguishable from interpretation.

02

Decision relevance

Every AI output maps to a real decision, action, or handoff—not a decorative insight.

03

Named next actor

Every recommendation states who acts next and does not imply unauthorized execution.

04

Visible state

Every status has a defined meaning, transition, owner, and allowed next state.

05

Exceptions surface

Ambiguity, missing information, and conflict become visible rather than being silently resolved.

06

Bounded authority

Every critical judgment is explicitly automated, assisted, or human-only, with the system of record kept clear.

Responsibility model

Human judgment stays visible

Forge defines not only what the product does, but also what the human owns and what the system of record preserves.

Responsibility Human Product / AI System of record
Capture Provides or confirms source information. Structures unstructured input and detects gaps. Stores raw and structured input.
Interpret Applies context and domain judgment. Summarizes, classifies, and explains detected signals. Stores interpretation and confidence.
Assess Owns final risk and readiness decision. Applies defined checks and surfaces exceptions. Records status, reason, and approval.
Act Approves, edits, communicates, or executes. Recommends next action and prepares the handoff. Tracks ownership and completion.
Audit Reviews history and outcomes. Preserves rationale, timestamps, evidence, and overrides. Maintains the durable record.
Artifact system

Evidence, not paperwork

Each artifact exists to resolve a decision, reduce uncertainty, or prepare the next validation or delivery step.

Decision gates

What must be true before moving forward

Forge uses explicit evidence thresholds to prevent enthusiasm, feature pressure, or architecture ambition from outrunning validation.

Qualified

Engage → Discover

The problem, access, value, product fit, and commercial path are credible.

  • Anchor stakeholder named
  • Observed problem stated without solution bias
  • Evidence sources and discovery boundaries identified

Defined

Model → Define

The current work, future operating motion, decision roles, data, and boundaries are understood.

  • Steel thread and thesis are explicit
  • Human authority and system responsibilities are clear
  • P0 capabilities trace to evidence

Validated

Prototype → Plan

Representative scenarios produce enough evidence to advance, refine, reframe, or pause.

  • Ready, ambiguous, blocked, exception, and edge cases covered
  • Workflow fit, trust, output quality, usability, and value scored
  • Recommendation is evidence-supported

Delivery-ready

Plan → Build

The architecture and backlog fit the current stage and make responsibilities, controls, failure handling, and migration triggers explicit.

  • System of record and automation authority defined
  • Acceptance tests and release gates mapped
  • Security, support, observability, and transition assumptions documented
